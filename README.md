# Growthskale Task 1 – E-Commerce Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a Data Analyst internship task at Growthskale.

The project focuses on analyzing an e-commerce sales dataset to understand overall business performance, identify high-performing and loss-making areas, evaluate regional performance, and examine the impact of discounting on profitability.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Objective

The objective of this analysis was to:

- Understand overall business performance
- Identify high-performing and low-performing categories and sub-categories
- Analyze sales and profit across regions and customer segments
- Examine the relationship between discounting and profitability
- Present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## Key Insights

- Total Sales: $2.30M
- Total Profit: $286K
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Furniture had a significantly lower profit margin (2.5%) compared with Technology and Office Supplies.
- Tables was the largest loss-making sub-category despite generating significant sales.
- Loss-making orders had a much higher average discount (48.1%) than profitable orders (8.1%).

## Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to present the key findings.

The dashboard includes KPI cards, regional and category analysis, sub-category analysis, and interactive slicers for Category, Region, and Segment.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that the business is profitable overall, but profitability is not evenly distributed across categories and sub-categories.

Furniture, particularly Tables, represents a major area of concern. The analysis also indicates that higher discounting is associated with loss-making orders.

Controlling excessive discounts and reviewing weaker-performing areas could help improve overall profitability without necessarily requiring an increase in total sales.
